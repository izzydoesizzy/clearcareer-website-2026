Hey {{ contact.FIRSTNAME | default: "friend" }},

Quick nudge. If you've already done the intake, ignore this and go enjoy your evening.

If you haven't, here's why it matters: everything we build in the next 8 weeks starts from that conversation. Resume, LinkedIn, target list, interview stories. All of it. The 30 minutes you spend talking is the 30 minutes that saves you 20 hours of homework.

Three tips for a better report:

  1. Say numbers, even rough ones. "A team of about 8," "roughly $2M budget," "maybe 40% faster." The report will ask you to confirm later. Rough beats blank.
  2. Tell stories, not duties. Not "I managed vendor relationships." Instead: "There was a vendor who kept missing deadlines, so I..." The achievements hide in the stories.
  3. Don't filter. If you think something isn't impressive, say it anyway. Clients are wrong about what's impressive about them roughly 100% of the time.
