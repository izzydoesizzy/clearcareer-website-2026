Saturday Newsletter · step 0Every Saturday, 7:30am Eastern
From: Izzy Piyale-Sheard <izzy@joinclearcareer.com>[Hook, under 50 characters, no clickbait][One line that extends the hook]

Happy Saturday friends!

[PERSONAL OPENER, 2 to 4 sentences. Something from Izzy's actual week: Reyhan, cycling, an ADHD moment, a client call that stuck, a thing he got wrong. Specific. One detail nobody else would have.]

[THE STORY OR METAPHOR, 2 to 4 short paragraphs. Either a named client story with before and after numbers, or an extended metaphor from life that lands a career lesson. The voice guide has the bank: the grocery store, quicksand, farming vs hunting, the anaconda, Tony Starking.]

Here's the tactic.

[ONE TACTIC, with exact steps. Name the tool, the button, the words. 3 to 5 numbered steps. Pair with one concrete example.]

Do this ONE thing this week: [a single micro-action that takes under 20 minutes]

[CTA BLOCK, one of four, rotating:]

[Week 1] Want 25 minutes with me on your search? It's free, it's also a sales call, and I say so up front. Book a Strategy Session

[Week 2] Want me in your corner every week for less than a dollar a day? Join the Community, $29/mo

[Week 3] This month's $9 tool: [name and one line]. Get it for $9

[Week 4] Free this week: [tool name and one line]. Try it

{% if contact.MEMBER == "yes" %} In the Community this week: [Wednesday's hot seat topic, this week's win from a member (first name only), the next workshop date.]

Have a great weekend,

Izzy

P.S. [A teaser for next week, a human detail, or a one-line reframe. Never a second CTA.]
