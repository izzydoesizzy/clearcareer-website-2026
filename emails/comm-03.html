Two weeks in. Quick check.

Reply with one line: what's working, and what isn't?

That's it. No form, no survey. I read every reply and I use them to decide what Wednesday's hot seats and next month's workshop should cover.

If the honest answer is "I haven't shown up yet," that's okay, and that's also useful. Tell me what got in the way. Half the time the fix is just picking a different call to make your anchor.

And if the honest answer is "this is great," I'd love to hear that too. I run this thing mostly on coffee and replies.

Izzy
