Deposit received. Let's get you paid.

First, the most important line in this email: do not reply to the employer yet. Not "thanks, I'll think about it," not a question about benefits, nothing. If they've asked for an answer, the only thing you send is: "Thank you, I'm excited about this. I'd like to take a couple of days to review everything properly. Can I come back to you by [day]?" That's it. We'll write everything else together.

Second, send me three things by reply or WhatsApp:

  1. The offer letter or email, exactly as they sent it.
  2. The job description and anything you know about the team, the manager, and how the interviews went.
  3. Your three numbers from the generator, if you've done it: anchor, target, walk-away. If you haven't, we'll do it on the call.

Third, book our 60-minute strategy call. The sooner the better; most negotiations have a 2 to 5 day window.

Book your 60-minute strategy call

On the call we'll clarify the role, position your value, set the numbers, and I'll draft the counteroffer email with you before we hang up. WhatsApp is open the whole time the negotiation is live.

Reminder of the deal: we add at least $5,000 to your offer or you pay nothing. The $2,000 balance is only due when you accept the better offer.

Izzy

P.S. Breathe. You have more room than you think. 94% of negotiated offers stay on the table, and the ones that get pulled were never real offers.
