Hey {{ contact.FIRSTNAME | default: "friend" }},

Noticed you've been reading the negotiation stuff. Two clicks in a month usually means one of two things: an offer is coming, or you're kicking yourself about the last one.

If an offer is coming, here's the fast version of everything I know:

  1. Never give the first number if you can help it. "I'd love to hear what range you've budgeted for the role" buys you time and information.
  2. If you must give a number, give your anchor, which is the top of the researched range, not the middle.
  3. Negotiate in writing when you can. Nerves don't show in an email.
  4. Everything is negotiable, not just base. Signing bonus, PTO, start date, remote days, a 6-month review.
  5. The first offer is never the final offer. 94% of negotiated offers stay on the table.

And if the offer is a $100K+ one, this is the exact situation the Salary Negotiation Sprint exists for: we add at least $5,000 or you pay nothing. The first step is a free 25-minute call, ideally this week, because these windows are short.
