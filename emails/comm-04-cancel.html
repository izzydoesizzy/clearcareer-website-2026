Community Onboarding · step 4When a member cancels
From: Izzy Piyale-Sheard <izzy@joinclearcareer.com>Before you goNo guilt trip. One question and one option.

Hey {{ contact.FIRSTNAME | default: "friend" }},

Saw you cancelled. No guilt trip, I promise. People cancel for good reasons, and the best one is "I got the job." If that's you: congratulations. Seriously. Reply and tell me, I want to celebrate it.

If it's something else, one question: what would have made this worth $29 to you? One line. I'm not going to argue, I'm going to listen. It's how the community gets better for the next person.

And one option you might not know about: you can pause instead of cancel. Reply "pause" and I'll freeze your membership for up to 3 months at no charge, so you keep your rate and your access to the recordings when you come back.

Either way, the Saturday email keeps coming, and the door's open.

Izzy
