The year I didn't get a single offer

Lead Welcome · step 2Day 1
From: Izzy Piyale-Sheard <izzy@joinclearcareer.com>The year I didn't get a single offerCareer zig-zagger. Jack of all trades. Here's how that became the point.

Hey {{ contact.FIRSTNAME | default: "friend" }},

I've never had a straight-line career. Events, partnerships, community, teaching, a bit of everything. For years I thought that was the problem.

It made my resume read like a buffet. Nobody could tell what I was for. And I spent long stretches unemployed, confused, and quietly convinced something was wrong with me. A full year without a single offer, at one point. Stuck in loops I couldn't break.

When I moved into tech I did what I always do: I devoured everything and showed up everywhere. And I noticed something. At every event, people responded to the exact things I'd been embarrassed about. The energy. The habit of connecting people. The "I'll figure it out" muscle.

That was the unlock for me. The mix I thought made me unfocused WAS the value. I just hadn't learned to say it.

Once I could, opportunities started bubbling up. Not from job boards. From people.

I've spent every year since 2016 building the support I wished I'd had back then. 200+ people coached. A 900+ member community. And a very specific belief: you are almost certainly more hireable than your resume says you are.

Tomorrow: the one small thing I'd buy if I were you (it's $9, and you can skip it).

Izzy
