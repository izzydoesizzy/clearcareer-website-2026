Let's do some math. I'll use $100K because it keeps the numbers clean. Swap in your own.

$100,000 a year is about $8,300 a month.

The average job search right now runs 5.5 months. For senior roles, 6 to 9.

So the "average" search costs somewhere between $45,000 and $75,000 in income you didn't earn. That's not a metaphor. That's a car. That's a down payment. That's a year of daycare.

And here's the part that stings: most of that time is spent doing the thing with the worst odds. Applying. Refreshing. Applying again.

I'm not telling you this to scare you. I'm telling you because when I show people this number, something shifts. They stop asking "how do I apply to more jobs?" and start asking "how do I cut two months off this?"

That second question has an answer. It's a system, not more effort. I'll show you the whole thing in a couple of days.

Izzy
