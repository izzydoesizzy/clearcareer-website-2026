Something you said on the call stuck with me: [OBJECTION. Default: "I've tried so much already, I'm not sure anything will be different."]

Jorge said almost exactly that. He'd been searching for 12 months. 160+ applications. 16 interviews. Zero offers. He was convinced the problem was him.

It wasn't. The interviews were happening, which meant the resume worked. The offers weren't, which meant the interview prep and the follow-up were where the search was leaking. So that's what we fixed. Not everything. The one leak.

Three weeks later he had an offer. Then the employer said they "couldn't go higher," and he got $6K more anyway, because we'd written the response before he needed it.

The lesson isn't "Jorge got lucky." It's that "I've tried everything" usually means "I've tried everything at the top of the funnel." The fix is almost always one stage, and you already know which one yours is. We found it on Tuesday.
