Blake sent 200 applications. Then 27 emails.

Hey {{ contact.FIRSTNAME | default: "friend" }},

Quick story.

Blake writes for a living. Sharp guy. He applied to 200+ roles over months. Tailored resumes, the works. He got a handful of interviews and no offers. Silence, mostly.

Then he stopped applying.

Instead, he picked the companies he actually wanted to work for and sent 27 emails. Not applications. Emails. To real people, about real things, asking for a conversation.

13 replied. Three of those turned into calls about roles. One of them hired him, $5K above what he was hoping for.

Same guy. Same experience. Same resume, more or less. The only thing that changed was where the effort went.

Here's the thing most people miss: your effort was never the problem. 200 applications is a LOT of effort. It just went into the part of the funnel with the worst odds.

Tomorrow I'll show you what that costs, in actual dollars. It's a bigger number than you think.

Izzy
