Hey {{ contact.FIRSTNAME | default: "friend" }},

Your group starts Monday. Here's the week.

Monday, 30 minutes, priorities call. We each name the three things we're doing this week. That's it. Short on purpose.

Wednesday, 60 minutes, group call. This is where the work happens. Resume drafts, LinkedIn headlines, the first version of your target list. Bring whatever you've got. Rough is fine. Rough is expected.

Between calls: WhatsApp. Weekday business hours, lighter evenings and weekends. Ask a question, share a draft, get unstuck. Don't sit on something for three days. Send it.

Your one assignment before Wednesday: read your 20-page report once, start to finish, with a pen. Circle anything that made you go "huh, I did do that." Bring the circles.

Everything is pinned in Skool: Zoom links, the week-by-week map, the template vault, the prompt library.

This is the part where it starts to feel less like a grind and more like a team sport. See you Monday.

Izzy
