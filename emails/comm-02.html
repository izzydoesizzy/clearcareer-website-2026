Bring one thing to Wednesday

Community Onboarding · step 2Day 3
From: Izzy Piyale-Sheard <izzy@joinclearcareer.com>Bring one thing to Wednesday (here's how to prep it in 10 minutes)Hot seats work when you bring something real. Here's what counts.

Hey {{ contact.FIRSTNAME | default: "friend" }},

Wednesday is the heart of this whole thing. Here's how to get the most out of your first one.

Bring ONE thing. Not your whole search. One of these:

  • A resume bullet you suspect is weak
  • A job posting you want and don't know how to approach
  • A LinkedIn message you drafted and didn't send
  • An interview question that keeps tripping you
  • An offer, a counter, or a conversation you need to have with a manager

Prep it in 10 minutes: paste it into a doc, add two lines of context (what it's for, what you've tried), and have it ready to screen-share. That's it.

Then raise your hand. The first hot seat is the scariest and the most useful. After that it's just Wednesday.

Add Wednesday to your calendar

Izzy

P.S. Can't make it live? Post your one thing in Skool with the tag "hot seat" and I'll cover it on the call. You get the recording the same day.
