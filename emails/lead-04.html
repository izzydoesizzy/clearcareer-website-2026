1 year unemployed. Hired in 3 weeks.

Lead Welcome · step 4Day 6
From: Izzy Piyale-Sheard <izzy@joinclearcareer.com>1 year unemployed. Hired in 3 weeks.Kristin and Sparsh. Experience was never the problem.

Hey {{ contact.FIRSTNAME | default: "friend" }},

Two quick ones.

Kristin had plenty of experience. She'd been looking for full-time work for an entire year. Her words: "even though I had a lot of experience, I was still stuck." We rebuilt her resume around results, changed how she approached companies, and she was employed in three weeks.

Sparsh had been searching for over a year. Hired in 30 days.

I share these two together because they have the same lesson, and it's not "Izzy is magic." It's this: a year of searching is not evidence that you're unhireable. It's evidence that the method isn't working. Those are very different problems, and the second one is fixable.

In the December 2024 survey of 58 members, 43% had been searching 7+ months before joining. Of the people who landed, 46% did it within one month. Not because they suddenly got more experience. Because the effort finally went to the right place.
