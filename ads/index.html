Ads Plan

How to spend on Meta ads using the Evelyn Weiss model: budget phases, targets, creatives, kill rules.Source: docs/ADS.md

How and when to spend on Meta ads, modelled on the way Evelyn Weiss grows low-ticket memberships, scaled down to a one-person business that has not run ads yet. The short version: earn the right to spend with organic first, then buy comments cheaply, then buy members directly, and never spend more than last month made.

1. What Evelyn Weiss actually does (documented)

Evelyn Weiss is Skool's Ads Manager and the person Izzy cited as the model for the community in the November 2025 notes. What is publicly documented about her approach:

  • She has built low-ticket memberships to over $9 million in revenue, with 33,000+ paid subscribers and 60,000+ low-ticket customers since 2020, and has spent over $25 million on Meta ads for subscriptions and memberships. She is a two-time Skool Games winner and, with Jessa Bellman, added about $81K in monthly recurring revenue in roughly 30 days in the first Skool Games.
  • Her model is cold traffic from Meta ads sent directly to the membership, not to a lead magnet first. She reports that sending ads straight to Skool using its conversion API and optimising for "complete registration" beat lead-generation campaigns on cost.
  • She uses a low-ticket front end to scale, priced so the decision is small ($7 to $29 a month in her own communities), with a 7-day free trial on her paid community.
  • Her documented free-community campaign spent €583 for 505 members, about $1.33 per member.
  • The pattern across her material: pick a narrow niche, deliver value before monetising, keep the offer cheap enough that an ad can sell it without a call, and let retention carry the economics.

Sources: Evelyn Weiss on Instagram, LinkedIn, her Skool community, her site, the Skool Games podcast episode, and the Skoolmakers review. Figures vary slightly between sources; the ranges above are the overlap.

Everything below section 1 is my application of that model to ClearCareer, not something Evelyn has said about ClearCareer.

2. Translating the model

Evelyn's model ClearCareer version
Cold ads straight to a low-ticket membership Cold ads straight to the Community page ($29/mo, 7-day free trial during the ad phase)
Optimise for registration or purchase, not leads Meta Sales campaign with Skool's Conversions API, optimising for Complete Registration (trial start)
A low-ticket front end as the first yes The $9 products, sold through comment-to-DM ads via ManyChat (cheaper per lead, and they identify buyers)
Retention carries the economics Weekly rhythm (Monday, Wednesday, monthly workshop), onboarding emails, pause instead of cancel
Narrow niche $100K+ professionals in Canada and the US, 3+ months into a search or holding an offer
Scale with spend Scale only when cost per paid member is under one third of 6-month member value

Two campaign types, in this order:

  1. Comment-to-DM ads (Phase B). Boost the reels that already earned comments organically. The ad's CTA is the keyword. ManyChat handles the DM. This buys keyword comments at $1 to $3 each and feeds the $9 funnel and the Strategy Session. It also teaches you which hooks work before you spend on the membership.
  2. Community conversion ads (Phase C). Evelyn's core play. Short talking-head ads to the Community page with the 7-day trial. Optimise for registration.

3. Budget by phase

Phase Weeks Daily budget Monthly Objective Go / no-go to the next phase
A: Organic only 1 to 4 $0 $0 3 reels a week, find 3 hooks with 50+ keyword comments or a 5%+ save rate At least 2 reels with 50+ comments, ManyChat flows tested, $9 products test-purchased
B: Buy comments 5 to 8 $10 $300 Engagement campaign on the top 3 reels, keyword CTA, ManyChat Cost per keyword comment under $1.50; cost per $9 buyer under $15; at least 1 Strategy Session booked from ad traffic
C: Buy members 9 to 16 $20 to $30 $600 to $900 Sales campaign to the Community page, 7-day trial, Complete Registration Cost per trial under $15; trial to paid 40%+; cost per paid member under $40
D: Scale 17+ +20% per week while rules hold $1,000+ Same two campaigns, add retargeting Cost per paid member under $58 (one third of 6-month value); cost per booked Session under $150

Hard caps:

  • Before the first core sale ($497 or more), ads are capped at $300 a month.
  • Ad spend in any month never exceeds last month's revenue minus fixed costs.
  • Any ad set that spends $100 without a single keyword comment, trial, or booking is paused the same day.

4. The unit economics to hold

Metric Value How it is derived
Community 6-month value about $174 $29 x 6 months, the conservative average retention target
Max cost per paid member $58 One third of 6-month value
Trial to paid 40%+ Industry norm for a 7-day trial with onboarding; below 30% means fix onboarding, not ads
So max cost per trial about $23 $58 x 40%
Expected value of a held Strategy Session about $700 35% close x blended sale of about $2,000 (mix of $497, $2,497, $4,997)
Max cost per booked Session $150 Assumes 70% show, so about $215 per held call, under one third of expected value
$9 buyer value in the first 60 days about $45 $9 + 30% bump at $19 + the Session booking rate applied to the $700 call value
Max cost per $9 buyer $15 One third of 60-day value

Read these every Monday from the scorecard in KPIS.md. When a number is outside its limit for two weeks, change the creative first, the audience second, the offer never (the offer is tested on calls, not in ads).

5. Audiences

Phase B (engagement): Instagram and Facebook placements, Reels only. Canada and the United States. Ages 28 to 55. Advantage+ audience seeded with: people who engaged with the Instagram account in the last 90 days, website visitors in the last 30 days, and the customer list (buyers). Interests as a fallback set: job search, LinkedIn, Indeed, Glassdoor, career coaching, laid off, Levels.fyi.

Phase C (conversion): same geography and age. Two ad sets: (1) Advantage+ seeded with buyers and website visitors, (2) a retargeting set of people who watched 50% of a reel in the last 14 days or visited the site in the last 30 days and did not join. Exclude current members and clients (upload the Brevo list of MEMBER=yes and CLIENT set as a custom audience, refreshed monthly).

Exclude always: people under 25, current clients, the free community members once that exists.

6. Creative

Rules: selfie camera, natural light, captions on, first line is the hook, one teach, one CTA. 15 to 45 seconds for Phase B, 30 to 60 seconds for Phase C. Three versions of each hook. Replace the bottom performer every two weeks.

Phase B ads (comment-to-DM)

Ad B1, SALARY. Hook: "I took the first number they offered for the first ten years of my career. That mistake cost me six figures." Body: "Here's the thing nobody tells you. 'What are you looking for?' is a test. Most people fail it in the first sentence, because they say a number before they know their three numbers: the anchor, the target, the walk-away. I built a 10-minute tool that gives you all three for your role and your city, plus the exact words to say. It's nine dollars." CTA (spoken and on screen): "Comment SALARY and I'll DM it to you." Primary text: "55% of people never negotiate. The ones who do get about 18% more. Comment SALARY for the 10-minute tool." Headline: "Your three numbers in 10 minutes."

Ad B2, OUTREACH. Hook: "200 applications is not a job search. It's a lottery ticket with worse odds." Body: "Blake writes for a living. 200+ applications, nothing. Then he sent 27 emails to real people at companies he wanted. 13 replied. One hired him. Same resume. I packaged the 30 scripts he used: cold email, LinkedIn ask, follow-up, referral request. Nine dollars." CTA: "Comment OUTREACH and I'll DM you the scripts." Primary text: "85% of jobs are filled through people. Here are the exact words. Comment OUTREACH." Headline: "30 scripts. 27 emails. 13 replies."

Ad B3, PROMPTS. Hook: "'Make my resume better' is the worst prompt you can type. Here's the one that works." Body (screen recording): paste the weak bullet, run the achievement-mining prompt, show the result with numbers. "Fifty of these, organised by stage. 98% of my clients who compared say they beat anything else. Nine dollars." CTA: "Comment PROMPTS." Primary text: "Before: 'Managed a team.' After: 'Led 12 engineers, shipped a $2.4M migration 3 weeks early.' One prompt. Comment PROMPTS." Headline: "50 prompts. One career strategist."

Phase C ads (Community)

Ad C1, the dollar a day. Hook: "Nobody prepares you for how lonely job searching actually is." Body: "Every Monday, 30 minutes, we set the week. Every Wednesday, 60 minutes, you bring the resume or the message you're stuck on and we fix it live. Monthly workshops. Every template I use with private clients. It's $29 a month. That's less than a dollar a day, and the first seven days are free." CTA: "Tap below to start your free week." Primary text: "Weekly coaching with a career coach who's helped 200+ people land roles and negotiate $1.2M+ in raises. $29/mo after a free week. Cancel anytime." Headline: "Weekly coaching for less than $1 a day."

Ad C2, the hot seat. Hook: "Watch me fix a resume bullet in 90 seconds." (screen recording from a real hot seat, with permission) Body: show the before, the three questions, the after. "This is Wednesday in the ClearCareer Community. Bring yours." CTA: "Start your free week." Primary text: "Live hot seats every Wednesday. Bring the thing you're stuck on. 7 days free, then $29/mo." Headline: "Bring one thing to Wednesday."

Ad C3, the contrast. Hook: "Job searching alone in 2020 vs. job searching with a room full of people who get it." Body: split screen. Left: refreshing job boards at 11pm. Right: a Wednesday call, someone's win in the chat. "46% of our members who landed did it within a month. Most had been stuck for over six months before." CTA: "Start your free week." Primary text: "A community that moves. Monday priorities, Wednesday hot seats, monthly workshops. Free for 7 days." Headline: "Stop job searching alone."

More hooks per pillar are in CONTENT-CALENDAR.md.

7. Tracking

  • Meta Pixel on every page of the new site (add the snippet to the shared head when Phase B starts).
  • Skool Conversions API connected from Skool settings before Phase C.
  • ManyChat's Instagram ads integration so comment-to-DM ads attribute to the ad.
  • UTMs on every ad URL: utm_source=meta_ads&utm_medium=ad&utm_campaign=<B1..C3>.
  • A weekly ads row in the scorecard: spend, keyword comments, DM opens, link clicks, $9 buyers, trials, paid members, Sessions booked, and the cost per each.

8. What we are not doing yet

  • Multiple low-ticket front ends at once. One keyword campaign at a time until Phase D.
  • Lead-generation forms. Evelyn's data says they lose to direct registration, and ours would lose to comment-to-DM.
  • Discounting the Community in ads. The trial is the offer. The price stays $29.
  • Spending before organic has found the hooks. Ads amplify what already works; they do not find it for you cheaply.
