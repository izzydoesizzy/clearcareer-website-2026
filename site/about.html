My story

Hi, I'm Izzy.

Career zig-zagger. Jack of all trades. For years I couldn't tell a straight story about what I did, let alone why anyone should pay me for it.

I went through long stretches of unemployment. Unclear, confused, unmotivated, stuck in loops I couldn't break. I know what it feels like to apply into silence for months and start wondering if the problem is you.

When I moved into tech, I devoured every resource I could find and showed up at every event. And something odd kept happening. People responded to the energy, the resourcefulness, and the habit of connecting people who should know each other.

Then it clicked. The mix of skills I thought made me unfocused was the value. Partnerships, community, events, mentorship, fast execution, problem-solving. Once I started telling that story, opportunities started bubbling up.

Since 2016 I've been building the support I wished I'd had when I was struggling in silence. That's what ClearCareer is. I don't hand you advice and send you home. We build it together.

The rest of the picture

Before ClearCareer I spent years working remotely from 20+ countries. A lot of my ideas about career design come from watching how differently people work when nobody is telling them where to sit.

I have an ADHD brain. That's not a footnote. It's the reason every system here looks the way it does: small doable steps, someone beside you, and no giant plan you'll abandon by Thursday. I build what I need to keep going, then I hand it to you.

Home is Toronto. I'm a dad, which means I now understand negotiation at a whole new level.
