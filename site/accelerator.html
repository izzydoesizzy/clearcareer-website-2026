Fit

Built for a specific kind of professional

I'd rather turn you away than sell you the wrong thing. So, honestly:

This is for you if

  • You're earning or targeting $100K+ in tech, product, data, engineering, finance, operations, or marketing
  • You've been laid off and the severance clock is ticking. You want to move strategically, not desperately
  • You've sent 50 to 200+ applications with little or nothing back. You're motivated. Your system is broken
  • You've worked with coaches who gave advice and left you alone with it. You want someone to build it with you
  • You want a group that will celebrate your first reply, challenge your excuses, and keep you moving
  • You're employed and searching quietly. No evening calls, nobody at work needs to know
  • You can commit 3 to 5 hours a week for 8 weeks

This is not for you if

  • You have fewer than 7 years of professional experience. The group is built for senior searches
  • You can't commit 3 to 5 hours a week right now. The system works, but only if you work it
  • You want a done-for-you service where someone else handles everything. This is done with you
  • You want solo, self-paced learning. The group is central to how this works
  • You're not targeting $100K+ roles. The assets and targeting are built for senior-level searches
  • You want a job guarantee. Nobody honest can promise you a job, and I won't pretend otherwise

If any of those are you right now, this isn't your moment, and that's okay. Start with the Community or the Clarity Intensive.

Questions

Eight things people ask before they join

Still got one? Ask it on the Strategy Session. That's what it's for.

Is this a group program or 1:1 coaching with Izzy?

Both. You get three private sessions with me plus eight weeks inside a group of up to 10 senior professionals running the same search. The group accountability is where most of the magic happens. Want weekly 1:1? That's the Private Accelerator.

How is this different from other coaches or resume services?

Most coaches give you advice and send you home to do it alone. Here everything gets built with you: resume written, LinkedIn drafted, outreach templates ready. You leave with 20+ real deliverables, not a to-do list.

What do the 8 weeks look like?

Week 1 is your AI Intake Interview and kickoff session with me. Weeks 2 to 7 are Monday priorities, Wednesday group calls, and WhatsApp support as you build and apply the system. Week 4 is your mid-program review. Week 8 is your closing call.

When does it start?

A new group starts the first Monday of every month, capped at 10. Book the Strategy Session, and if it's a fit you'll be in the next start.

I'm not in Canada. Can I join?

Yes. Everything is remote. Clients are in Canada, the US, and internationally. Prices are in CAD.

Do I need to be actively job searching?

No. Many members are employed and quietly exploring. If you're preparing to search within three months, you're ready.

What if I don't see results?

Nobody can promise you a job. I do promise you'll leave with a complete, professional-grade system built for your search, and if you finish the 8 weeks without interviews I keep coaching you free until you have them. Members using this system have landed roles in 3 to 8 weeks after 6 to 12 months stuck.

I've tried programs that didn't work. Why is this different?

Because most programs give you content. This one builds assets. And the group holds you accountable where solo courses fail. Andrew paid for expensive courses that went nowhere, then had employer replies in his first week here.
