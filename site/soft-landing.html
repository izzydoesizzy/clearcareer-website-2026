The problem

Why traditional outplacement fails

Only about 40% of employees who are offered outplacement ever use it. Here's why. The standard experience is a portal login, a library of pre-recorded webinars, and a coach juggling 75 to 100 people at once.

Nobody logs in twice. So the company pays for a benefit, the employee gets nothing, and the Glassdoor review writes itself.

We built Soft Landing to be the thing people use.
