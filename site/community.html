The weekly rhythm

Two calls a week. One workshop a month. Everything recorded.

A job search without a rhythm turns into doom-scrolling job boards at 11pm. This is the rhythm.

01

Monday priorities call · 30 min

Pick your three moves for the week. Say them out loud to people who'll ask about them on Wednesday. Go.

02

Wednesday hot seat · 60 min

Bring a resume, a job post, a stuck message. We fix it live, in front of everyone, and everyone learns from it. This is the heart of the Community.

03

Monthly workshop · first Thursday

One topic, deep. Rotating through LinkedIn, interviews, negotiation, and AI. Bring questions.

04

The vault and the feed · always on

The template vault, the 30+ prompt library, every call recording, and the Skool feed where the questions get answered between calls.

Questions

Seven things people ask before they join

Anything else? Email Izzy and ask.

Are the other members serious?

Yes. They're paying $29 a month to work on their search every week, and the Wednesday hot seats fill up with real resumes and real job posts. Nobody's here to lurk. If you show up, you'll be in good company.

How much time does it take?

The Wednesday call is the heart: 60 minutes. Everything else is on your schedule. Monday is 30 minutes if you want it, and every call is recorded for when life gets in the way.

What time are the calls?

Eastern Time, scheduled for the time zones most members live in. Every call is recorded and posted the same day, so you never lose a week because of a meeting.

Is there a course?

No. There's no video course to fall behind on. You get the templates, the prompts, and the recordings, and they exist to support a real search. The value is the live coaching. That's the part you can't download.

What's the refund policy?

30 days. If you're not making real progress, I refund you. After that, cancel anytime in Skool and you won't be billed again. Details on the refunds page.

I'm brand new to job searching. Is this for me?

It works best if you're actively searching or about to start. If you're a year out, start with the free tools and the Saturday email, and join when you're ready to send things.

Will the price change?

$29 is the founding rate, for now. Members keep their rate for as long as they stay. If it goes up later, it goes up for new members, not for you.
