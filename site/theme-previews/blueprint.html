The problem

You did everything right. So why is nothing happening?

You tailored every resume. Fixed your LinkedIn. Applied to hundreds of roles. Then: silence. Here's the thing. Your effort isn't the problem. Your system is.

  • 01

    5.5 months on average

    That's the average job search right now (BLS, 2025). Senior roles run 6 to 9 months. At $100K, every one of those months costs you about $8,300.

  • 02

    200 applications, 17 callbacks

    About 8.5% of applications get any reply at all (Indeed, 2024). Most resumes never reach a human. More volume is not the fix.

  • 03

    85% of jobs go through people

    Referred candidates get hired at 10x the rate of applicants. Most job seekers spend 95% of their time on job boards and 5% talking to humans.

We flip that ratio. Then we build everything you need to make it work.

Questions

Five things people ask before they book

Everything else is answered on the Strategy Session page, including every price.

What happens on the Strategy Session?

For about 15 minutes I ask questions and find where your search is breaking: targeting, outreach, interviews, or the offer itself. Then I tell you what I'd build with you. And then, yes, I make you an offer. If it's a fit, I'll tell you which program and what it costs ($497 to $4,997) and ask if you want to do it. You can say no. People do, and we stay friends.

Is this group coaching or 1:1?

Both. The Accelerator is a group of up to 10 with three private sessions with me. The Private Accelerator is weekly 1:1. The Salary Negotiation Sprint and the Clarity Intensive are 1:1 by nature.

Do you work with people outside Canada?

Yes. Everything is remote. Clients are in Canada, the US, and a few other time zones. Prices are in Canadian dollars.

Do I need to be actively searching?

No. Many clients are employed and quietly exploring. If you're planning to search within three months, you're ready.

Is there a guarantee?

Nobody honest can promise you a job. Here's what I do promise. Finish the 8-week Accelerator without landing interviews and I keep coaching you at no cost until you do. On the Salary Negotiation Sprint, we add at least $5,000 to your offer or you pay nothing. The Community has a 30-day refund.
