Refund and Cancellation Policy

Effective October 2026.

Template drafted for ClearCareer. Have a lawyer review before publishing.

Short version: if something isn't working, email Izzy. Here's the policy for each offer, so there are no surprises later. All amounts are in Canadian dollars.

$9 to $67

Digital products

30 days, no forms. Salary Number Generator, Career Prompt Vault, Cold Outreach Script Pack, Job Search Script Vault, and the rest of the Shop. If it's not useful, email Izzy within 30 days of buying and we refund you. No "tell us why" quiz. You keep the files.

$29/mo or $249/yr

ClearCareer Community

30-day refund, then cancel anytime. In your first 30 days, if you're not making real progress, email us and we refund you in full. After that, cancel whenever you like. Your access runs to the end of the month or year you've paid for, and no further charges happen. We don't refund the unused part of a month or year.

$497

Career Clarity Intensive

Full refund before the AI Intake Interview. Change your mind any time before you complete the AI Intake Interview and we refund the full $497. After you complete the interview, the session is non-refundable, because the work has been done: your report is being written and your 90 minutes with Izzy are booked. If you'd rather reschedule the session, just ask. And the $497 still credits toward the Accelerator if you enrol within 30 days.

$2,497 and $4,997

Accelerator and Private Accelerator

Full refund before kickoff. After kickoff, the guarantee. Up to your kickoff session, you can leave and we refund everything you've paid. Once kickoff has happened, there's no refund. The interview guarantee applies instead: finish the 8 weeks without landing interviews and coaching continues at no cost until you do. If you're on a payment plan, the remaining instalments are still due after kickoff.

$2,497

Salary Negotiation Sprint

$5,000 added or you pay nothing. You pay a $497 deposit to start. The $2,000 balance is due when you accept the improved offer. If the gain is under $5,000 CAD, the balance is waived and the deposit is refunded. If you decide not to negotiate at all after our strategy call, the deposit covers the work done and isn't refunded.

For employers

Soft Landing

Per contract. Terms are set in each engagement contract. The default: you only pay for employees who complete intake and attend their kickoff session. Nobody who doesn't start is billed.

How to request a refund

  1. Email izzy@joinclearcareer.com from the address you used to order, or include that address in the message.
  2. Say which product or program it's for. One line is enough. You don't have to explain why, though if something was broken we'd love to know.
  3. We confirm by email and issue the refund through Stripe. It lands on your original payment method within 5 to 10 business days, depending on your bank.

Cancelling the Community

Cancel from your Skool account settings, or email us and we'll do it for you the same day. You'll keep access until the end of the period you've paid for.

Questions

If your situation doesn't fit neatly into one of the boxes above, email Izzy anyway. We'd rather talk it through than hide behind a policy.
