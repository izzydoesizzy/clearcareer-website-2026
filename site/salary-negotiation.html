Questions

Six things people ask before they negotiate

Will I lose the offer if I negotiate?

Almost never. A company that spent weeks interviewing you wants to close you, not restart the search. And we never bluff. Every number we put in writing is one you can back up, which is why the conversation stays warm.

What if they say "we can't go higher"?

Jorge heard exactly that. He got $6K more. "We can't go higher" usually means "not on base". The response to that line is drafted before you send your first email, and it opens the other doors: signing bonus, PTO, stipends.

How fast does this move?

First call within 48 hours if your offer is live. Most negotiations close in 5 to 10 days from that call. If they've given you a deadline, tell me on the Strategy Session and we work backwards from it.

Do you talk to the employer?

No. You do. Every word is written with you, in your voice, before you send it. They hired you, and they should hear from you.

Does it work for raises, not just offers?

Yes. Same system: anchor, target, walk-away, and the ask in writing. The guarantee applies to a documented raise.

What if the gain comes in under $5,000?

Then the $2,000 balance is waived and your $497 deposit is refunded. You keep the raise. That's the whole deal, and it's in writing before we start.
