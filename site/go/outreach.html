What's inside

30 scripts. Each one has the subject line, the body, and the one line you personalise.

  • 5 cold emails to a hiring manager. Mutual interest, a specific idea for their team, "[name] suggested I reach out", the company fan, and the direct ask. All under 150 words.
  • 5 LinkedIn connection requests, each under 300 characters. Shared background, content compliment, the recruiter connect, the company target, and the event follow-up.
