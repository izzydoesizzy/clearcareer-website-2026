Privacy Policy

Effective October 2026.

Template drafted for ClearCareer. Have a lawyer review before publishing.

ClearCareer is run by Izzy Piyale-Sheard in Toronto, Canada. This page says what we collect about you, why we collect it, which tools see it, and how to get it changed or deleted. Plain words where we can manage it.

What we collect

  • Your name and email. When you download a free tool, book a call, buy something, or join the Community.
  • Purchase details, through Stripe. What you bought, when, how much, and the last four digits of your card. Stripe holds the full card number. We never see it.
  • Booking details, through Calendly. The time you picked, your time zone, and the answers you give on the booking form.
  • Messages you send on Instagram, through ManyChat. If you reply to one of our automated DMs or type a keyword, we see the message and your Instagram handle.
  • What you share in coaching. Your resume, LinkedIn, target roles, salary history, offers. We keep it because we need it to do the work with you.
  • Usage analytics. Pages visited, roughly where from, what device and browser. Aggregated. Not tied to your name.

Why we collect it

  • To deliver what you bought: the access link, the files, the sessions.
  • To send the emails you asked for. Every one has an unsubscribe link at the bottom.
  • To run the Community and the programs: calls, recordings, the group chat, your progress.
  • To improve the site: which pages people read, which ones they leave, which links get clicked.

Nothing on that list should surprise you. If it does, email us and we'll explain or stop.

The tools we use and what they see

We're a small business. We don't build our own payment system or email server. These companies handle pieces of it, and each has its own privacy policy.

  • Stripe (payments): your name, email, card, and what you bought.
  • Brevo (email): your name, email, which emails you open, and which links you click.
  • Calendly (booking): your name, email, time zone, and booking form answers.
  • ManyChat and Meta (Instagram DMs): your Instagram handle and the messages you send us there.
  • Skool (the Community): your name, email, profile, posts, and comments inside the Community.
  • Zoom (calls): your name, video and audio during calls, and recordings where we tell you we're recording.
  • GitHub Pages (hosts this site): standard server logs such as IP address and pages requested.
  • Google Analytics or similar (usage): anonymised page views and traffic sources.

What we never do

  • Sell your data. Not to recruiters, not to advertisers, not to anyone.
  • Share your resume, your coaching notes, or anything you tell us in a session with anyone outside ClearCareer, unless you ask us to in writing.
  • Put your name, photo, or story on this site or in our marketing without asking you first.

Your rights

You can ask us, at any time, to:

  • Access what we hold about you.
  • Correct anything that's wrong.
  • Delete your data. We'll keep only what tax law says we must.
  • Unsubscribe. Every email has the link. Or reply and say "stop" and we'll do it by hand.

Email izzy@joinclearcareer.com from the address we have on file. We reply within 30 days, and usually within a few business days.

How long we keep it

  • Email list: until you unsubscribe.
  • Purchase records: as long as Canadian tax law requires, which is six years from the end of the tax year they relate to.
  • Coaching files and session notes: 12 months after your program ends, then deleted, unless you ask us to keep them longer or delete them sooner.
  • Call recordings: available to the people who were on the call, and deleted 12 months after the program ends.

Cookies

The site uses the cookies its analytics tool needs to count visits, and nothing else from us. Stripe, Calendly, and Skool set their own cookies on their own pages. You can block cookies in your browser and the site will still work.

Children

ClearCareer is for working professionals. It's not for anyone under 18. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone under 18, and if we learn we have, we delete it.

Changes to this policy

When we change this page, we update the effective date at the top. If a change is material, we'll email the list before it takes effect.

Governing law

This policy and how we handle your data are governed by the laws of Ontario and the federal laws of Canada that apply there, including PIPEDA.

Contact

Questions, requests, complaints: izzy@joinclearcareer.com. It goes to Izzy, not a ticket queue.
