What we sell

  • Digital products ($9 to $67 CAD): the Salary Number Generator, the Career Prompt Vault, the Cold Outreach Script Pack, the Job Search Script Vault, and the other guides in the Shop. Delivered as a web access page plus a PDF.
  • ClearCareer Community: $29 a month or $249 a year. Weekly live calls, the member community, and the resource library, for as long as you stay a member.
  • Career Clarity Intensive: $497. A 30-minute AI Intake Interview, a written career report, and a 90-minute session with Izzy. The $497 is credited toward the Accelerator if you enrol within 30 days.

  • Private Accelerator: $4,997, or 3 payments of $1,749. The same eight weeks, 1:1, with weekly private sessions.
  • Salary Negotiation Sprint: $2,497. A $497 deposit to start and $2,000 when you accept the improved offer.

What each one includes is described on its own page on this site. Those pages are part of these terms.

  • All prices are in Canadian dollars. Payments go through Stripe. We never see your full card number.

  • Payment plans (3 x $899 or 3 x $1,749) charge one instalment a month, starting the day you enrol. A payment plan is a way to spread the cost, not a month-to-month membership. Once your kickoff session has happened, all three instalments are owed, whether or not you keep showing up. If a payment fails, we'll email you and retry. If you're in a tough spot, tell us. We'd rather work something out than chase you.

  • Prices can change. The price you paid is the price you paid; a change only affects your next renewal or your next purchase.

Guarantees, and what nobody can guarantee

Here's what we promise:

  • Job Search Accelerator and Private Accelerator. Finish the 8 weeks without landing interviews and Izzy keeps coaching you at no cost until you do. "Finish" means you completed the AI Intake Interview, attended the calls, and built the assets with us.
  • Salary Negotiation Sprint. We add at least $5,000 CAD to your offer or you pay nothing: if the gain is under $5,000, the $2,000 balance is waived and the $497 deposit is refunded.
  • ClearCareer Community. 30-day refund if you're not making real progress.
  • Digital products. 30-day refund. Email Izzy. No forms.
  • Career Clarity Intensive. Full refund any time before you complete the AI Intake Interview.

Here's what nobody can promise, and we don't: a job, an interview with a specific company, or a specific salary. Hiring decisions belong to employers. Your results depend on your effort, your market, your timing, and things neither of us control. The client results on this site are real and self-reported, and they're not a prediction about yours.

Your responsibilities

  • Do the work. The programs are done with you, not for you. If we build a target list together, you send the messages.
  • Show up. Be on the calls you've booked. If you need to miss one, tell us before, not after. Sessions missed without notice may not be rescheduled.
  • Be honest. About your situation, your numbers, your offers, and what you've done since the last call. We can only coach what we know.
  • Keep your login and the materials to yourself. Don't record calls without asking everyone on them first.

Community conduct

The Community and the group programs work because people feel safe in them. So:

  • Treat everyone with respect. Disagree with ideas, not people.

If you break these, we'll remove you. If that happens after the 30-day refund window, there's no refund for the rest of the period.
