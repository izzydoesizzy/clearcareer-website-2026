KPIs and Tracking

The weekly scorecard, how each number is measured, and what to do when one is off.Source: docs/KPIS.md

One Google Sheet, four tabs, reviewed every Monday morning for 20 minutes. Every number has a definition, a source, a target, and the move to make when it is off.

2. The Sessions tab

One row per booked Strategy Session. Columns: Date · Name · Email · Source · Q1 situation · Q2 target role and salary · Q3 months searching · Q5 budget answer · Prep form done · Showed · Applications · Conversations · Interviews · Offers · Diagnosis · Offer made · Price · Outcome · Objection · Next step date · Closed value · Notes.

Monthly questions this tab answers: Which source books calls that close? Which diagnosis is most common? Which objection keeps appearing? Is Q5 screening the right people?

3. The Ads tab

One row per ad set per week: Phase · Campaign · Ad set · Spend · Impressions · CPM · Keyword comments · Cost per comment · Link clicks · $9 buyers · Cost per buyer · Trials · Cost per trial · Paid members · Cost per member · Sessions booked · Cost per booking · Decision (keep / swap creative / pause).

4. The Content tab

One row per reel: Date · Keyword · Hook (verbatim) · Tactic · Views at day 3 · Saves · Keyword comments · Comments per 1,000 views · Decision (keep / reshoot). Keep anything above 10 comments per 1,000 views.

6. Definitions that avoid arguments with yourself

  • A "qualified" booking answered Q5 with Yes or Partner. "Not right now" bookings are released and not counted.
  • A "show" is a person who joined the call for at least 10 minutes.
  • A "close" counts in the week the money arrives, not the week of the call.
  • "Core" is $2,497 or more. The $497 Clarity counts in close rate but not in core sales.
  • "Net members" is paying members at Sunday midnight, after cancellations.
  • Ad cost per result uses Meta's reported spend and your own counted results (ManyChat, Stripe, Skool, Calendly), never Meta's attributed conversions alone.
