Stage 2: One product, one workshop

New this stage: a Stripe Payment Link, a Drive folder as the access link, a Brevo form on the thank-you page, the Buyer Welcome automation, a hosted workshop registration form with three emails, and the workshop itself.

Why the workshop is the lead engine for you: it is live, it has a date, it is social, and it is the same talk every month. One FlexJobs session produced 30 testimonials. It uses the thing you are naturally good at and asks for nothing daily.

Tools: Brevo, Stripe, Google Drive and Meet, the thank-you page in this repository (GitHub Pages). Still no ManyChat, no Make, no new site on the domain.

You, weekly: Friday 30, Monday 20, replies, calls. Monthly: the workshop, 90 minutes including setup.

Chunks: 2.1 to 2.11. Do the product lane first (2.1 to 2.7), then the workshop lane (2.8 to 2.10). Each lane is about two evenings.

Move to Stage 3 when: two of the three numbers above. If only the workshop works, skip Stage 3 and run the workshop every month. That alone is a business.

Month 4 and beyond · only if you want more

Stage 4: Only if you want more

Everything built in this repository is waiting here: the site, the other 40 emails, the ads plan, the community rhythm, the KPI sheet, the delivery SOPs. None of it is required for the engine to run. All of it is ready when the engine is running and you want it to run bigger.

The honest trade: Stage 3 at full tilt is about 2.5 hours a week and produces a business. Stage 4 is a second job until the VA arrives. Choose it with your eyes open.

The only rule: never start Stage 4 to fix a Stage 1 problem. If the Saturday email is not going out, more channels will not help.
