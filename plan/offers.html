2. Each offer in detail

2.1 The $9 quick wins

Three products at launch. Each maps to an Instagram keyword and each gets the same order bump.

Keyword Product Inside Bump
SALARY The Salary Number Generator Anchor, target, and walk-away worksheet; research steps with the exact sites; the "what are you looking for?" script; the counteroffer email; the response to "we can't go higher"; the compounding math Job Search Script Vault, $19 (list $29)
PROMPTS Career Prompt Vault 50 prompts by job search stage: resume and skills, LinkedIn, market and company research, outreach, interview prep, negotiation and transitions; install guide for any AI tool; a "which prompt when" map Script Vault, $19
OUTREACH Cold Outreach Script Pack 30 scripts: cold email to a hiring manager, LinkedIn connection request, informational interview ask, follow-ups, referral request, recruiter reply, thank-you; each with subject, body, and the line to personalise Script Vault, $19

Phase 2 keywords: ADHD (ADHD Job Seeker's Focus Kit $9, bump ADHD Career Change Workbook $17) and LAYOFF (free Layoff Checklist, then the $67 Layoff Survival Kit, which already has a working upsell email).

Delivery: Stripe Payment Link with the bump as an optional item. After payment, Stripe redirects to the thank-you page and Make adds the buyer to Brevo, which sends the access email within a minute. Access is a private page or PDF folder per product. 30-day refund.

Why one bump for all three: one Stripe price to create, one message to learn, one number to read. Revisit after 60 days.

2.2 ClearCareer Community, $29/mo or $249/yr

Promise: weekly coaching with Izzy for less than a dollar a day.

The weekly rhythm (this fixes the "unclear value" problem from the November 2025 notes):

When What Length
Monday Priorities call: everyone names three things for the week 30 min
Wednesday Hot seat: bring a resume, a job post, a stuck message; fixed live 60 min
First Thursday Workshop, rotating LinkedIn, interviews, negotiation, AI 60 min
Always Template vault, 30+ prompt library, recordings, Skool feed

Includes: done-for-you email and networking templates, AI prompt library, video feedback on resume and LinkedIn, monthly challenges, guest speakers during community hours, recordings of everything.

Guarantee: 30 days. Cancel anytime after. Pause for up to 3 months instead of cancelling.

Pricing decisions: one price everywhere. Retire $49/mo, $249 lifetime, and $79/yr. "Normally $49" may appear as a strike-through for the first 90 days, then drop it. Existing lifetime members keep their access.

Launch rule: Skool costs $99/mo. Do not pay for an empty room. Pre-sell 10 founding members at $29 (or run the first month inside the free WhatsApp group) and open Skool when 4 are committed (break-even). See TOOLS-AND-COSTS.md.

2.3 Career Clarity Intensive, $497

New. Packages what already happens in a first session (Adam, Kevin, Marsha Malcolm, Veronica, Victor Rivas all describe one session that reframed their direction) with the AI Intake Interview.

Includes: the AI Intake Interview (30-minute voice) and the 20-page report (quantified achievements, 5 to 8 alternative job titles, 15 to 20 target companies, salary ranges, positioning statement); a 90-minute 1:1 with Izzy producing a 30-day plan; two weeks of WhatsApp follow-up; a $497 credit toward the Accelerator or Private Accelerator for 30 days after the session.

Timeline: buy, intake link within an hour, interview whenever, report within 3 business days, 90-minute session, two weeks of WhatsApp.

Who: "I don't know what I want next." "I can't spend $2,500 right now but I want Izzy's brain on it." Founders and zig-zaggers. People returning after a break. The budget "no" on the Strategy Session.

Refund: full refund before the intake interview is completed; non-refundable after, because the work has been done.

Capacity: 6 a month at first. Each one is 90 minutes of Izzy plus the automated intake.

2.4 Job Search Accelerator (group), $2,497 or 3 x $899

The JSIS program with four changes.

  1. Plain name in public. "The Job Search Accelerator." The system inside keeps the name Job Search Ignition System.
  2. Monthly starts. Groups of up to 10 start the first Monday of every month. Week 1 (intake and kickoff) is individual anyway. No more dead cohort dates.
  3. Price unchanged. The outcomes data was produced at this price. Clients call it under-priced.
  4. Guarantee unchanged. Finish the 8 weeks without landing interviews and Izzy keeps coaching at no cost until you do.

Includes: AI Intake Interview and report; the 20+ assets across Foundation, Target Strategy, Outreach System, and Interview Toolkit (full list in SITE-BUILD-GUIDE.md); 3 private sessions (60-min kickoff week 1, 45-min review week 4, 60-min closing week 8); Monday 30-min priorities call; Wednesday 60-min group call; WhatsApp weekdays; private community; weekly voice notes; 30+ prompt library; LinkedIn Content System (1 to 2 posts a week written for you).

Refund: full refund before the kickoff session. After kickoff, the interview guarantee applies instead.

Capacity: 10 per month. At full capacity this is $24,970 a month before payment plans.

2.5 Salary Negotiation Sprint, $2,497

The Notion "Salary Negotiation Package" ($2,500 USD) made public, priced in CAD, with the guarantee made concrete.

Guarantee: we add at least $5,000 CAD to your initial offer (base, signing bonus, equity at face value, extra PTO at your daily rate, or stipends) or you pay nothing.

Payment: $497 to start. $2,000 due when the improved offer is accepted. If the gain is under $5,000, the balance is waived and the deposit refunded.

Includes: 60-minute strategy and review call within 48 hours; role clarification and value positioning; anchor, target, and walk-away numbers; the counteroffer email drafted together; the response to the employer's counter drafted; unlimited WhatsApp during the live negotiation (usually 5 to 10 days).

Who: offer in hand or final round, $100K+ roles. Salary, hourly rate, or contract value. Raise negotiations too (guarantee applies to a documented raise).

Why it gets its own rung: shortest sales cycle you have (people with offers decide in 48 hours), clearest ROI, deepest testimonial bank (Septembre, Marsha D., Wil, Jorge, Kira, Tamara, Chris, Alison, Laura, Henrique), and the SALARY keyword surfaces these people every week. Nobody at this price offers a guarantee.

2.6 Private Accelerator, $4,997 or 3 x $1,749

Everything in the Accelerator, plus weekly private sessions instead of three, priority WhatsApp, mock interviews and custom interview coaching, LinkedIn posts written weekly, and the Salary Negotiation Sprint included when the offer lands.

Who: directors and above, laid-off senior people with a severance clock, anyone who wants zero group component.

Capacity: 3 new clients a month. Sold only on the call.

2.7 Profile Overhaul, $1,997 (call-only)

Resume with 15 to 20 CAR achievements, professional summary, LinkedIn headline in 3 versions, About section, custom banner, AI-enhanced headshot, setup call. Prescribe it when strategy is solid and the paper trail is weak. Not listed on the site because it competes with the Accelerator for attention and attracts the "just fix my resume" buyer. Merges the Notion "Resume + LinkedIn + Brand Overhaul, $2,500 USD" into one CAD price.

2.8 Soft Landing (employers)

Outplacement for companies doing layoffs. Starter $1,500 (30 days, 1 session), Essentials $3,000 (60 days, 2 sessions), Complete $5,000 (90 days, 3 sessions), Executive $8,000 (6 months, 5 sessions), per activated employee. Zero-cost retainer, you only pay for people who complete intake and attend kickoff. Own page (site/soft-landing.html), footer link, sold through LinkedIn and HR referrals. Out of scope for the consumer funnel.
