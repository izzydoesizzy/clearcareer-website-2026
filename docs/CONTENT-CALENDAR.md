# Content Calendar

Ninety days of content that feeds the funnel. Built around a fixed weekly template so the only creative decision each week is which hook to shoot.

## 1. The weekly template

| Day | Slot | Format | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tuesday | Reel A | Talking head or screen recording, 20 to 45s, keyword CTA | Feed a $9 funnel |
| Thursday | Reel B | Talking head, 20 to 45s, keyword CTA (different keyword) | Feed a $9 funnel |
| Saturday | Reel C | Personal, humour, or a client win. No keyword. | Reach and trust |
| Saturday | Carousel | 5 to 7 slides: a client result card, a "5 scripts" list, or a before/after | Saves and shares |
| Daily | 1 to 3 stories | Behind the scenes, a poll, a DM screenshot with permission, "reply SALARY" | Keep the DM channel warm |
| Saturday 7:30 ET | Newsletter | The Saturday email (template in `emails/`) | Nurture, one rotating CTA |
| Every reel | Cross-post | LinkedIn (video plus the full script as text), YouTube Shorts | Free reach on the channel with 4,000+ followers |

Batch day: one 3-hour block a week. Shoot 3 reels, cut them in CapCut, write the 3 captions and the LinkedIn text, build the carousel in Canva, schedule. The Saturday email is written Friday morning in 45 minutes.

## 5. Stories prompts (rotate)

- Poll: "Have you ever negotiated a job offer?" Yes / Never. Follow with "Reply SALARY and I'll send the generator."
- Screen: the Sessions sheet with names blurred: "Three Strategy Sessions this week. Two had offers in hand."
- Behind the scenes: the batch-day setup, the lunchbox note, the Wednesday hot seat (with permission).
- DM screenshot (permission): a first reply someone got using a script.
- Countdown: "New Accelerator group starts Monday. 3 spots."
- Question box: "What's the hardest part of your search right now?" Answers become next week's reels.

## 7. Measuring content

Per reel, read on day 3: views, saves, keyword comments, DM openers, link clicks. The number that matters is keyword comments per 1,000 views. Keep anything above 10. Reshoot anything below 3 with a different hook on the same idea.
